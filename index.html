A three-day, non-highway loop built around a strict 2-hour-leg cadence — 3 riding legs a day (breakfast → lunch → dinner), each capped around 55–60 miles at realistic twisty-backroad pace (~28–30 mph average, not car-routing speed). Covers the Kancamagus, Hurricane Mountain Road, Height of Land, Evans Notch, and the Rangeley Lakes region — with no road repeated across the three days.

Logistics to keep in mind

  • Fill up fully in Lincoln — the Kancamagus has zero gas stations for 34.5 miles.
  • Hurricane Mountain Road is paved but narrow, patchy, and twisty with real elevation change — take it easy, especially loaded down with luggage.
  • New lodging needed in Bethel, ME — nothing booked here yet.
  • ~204 miles total, the day's longest — Leg 3 runs a bit over 2h to fit in Hurricane Mountain Rd before Bethel.

Packing the T-Bag

  • Bottom / center, near the seat: tools, tire kit, toiletries — the heavy stuff, kept low and centered so it doesn't affect handling.
  • Top roll: clothing — light, compressible, fine to sit higher.
  • Outer pocket: rain gear, snacks, charging cable — anything you want at a gas stop without digging through the whole bag.
