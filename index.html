A three-day, non-highway loop built for twisty riding: the Kancamagus, Height of Land, Evans Notch, and the Rangeley Lakes region — with no road repeated across the three days.

1FRI

Friday · Sept 4 · Depart 8–9am

Kancamagus & Height of Land

12 Lincoln St, Waltham → Milford, NH → Lincoln, NH → Kancamagus Hwy → Rumford, ME → Height of Land → Rangeley

Route map

🗺️ Open Interactive Map in Google Maps ↗

Why this way

  • Milford's Riverhouse Cafe is an easy first stop on quiet NH backroads out of Waltham.
  • Loon Mountain into the Kancamagus Highway — the single best stretch of curves in New England.
  • Height of Land on Rt 17 is one of New England's most famous overlooks, with sweeping views across the Rangeley Lakes, and Rt 17 itself is remote and lightly trafficked.

Stops along the way

  • Milford, NHfood
  • Lincoln, NHfill up — mandatory
  • Conway, NHgasfood
  • Rumford, MEgas
  • Rangeley Saddleback Innovernight

Logistics to keep in mind

  • Fill up fully in Lincoln — the Kancamagus has zero gas stations for 34.5 miles.
  • ~230 miles total — the longest day of the trip, budget the full 7–8hr riding window.
  • Services thin out north of Rumford, so treat that as your last "sure thing" gas stop before Rangeley.
  • Book: Rangeley Saddleback Inn ↗ — request the Queen Room with Two Queen Beds.

Saturday · Sept 5 · Depart 8–9am

The Androscoggin & Evans Notch

Rangeley → Oquossoc → Errol, NH → Berlin → Gorham → Evans Notch → North Conway

Route map

🗺️ Open Interactive Map in Google Maps ↗

Why this way

  • Heading west out of Rangeley via Oquossoc and Errol follows the Androscoggin River valley — completely fresh scenery, with zero overlap with yesterday's Rt 17 climb.
  • Evans Notch is quiet, remote, and sees a fraction of the Kancamagus's traffic — a narrow, twisty canyon road bordering Maine and NH.

Stops along the way

  • Rangeleygas before departure
  • Errol, NHlight food
  • Berlin, NHgasfood
  • Gorham, NHfill up — mandatory
  • Colonial Motel, North Conwayovernight

Logistics to keep in mind

  • Fill up fully in Gorham — Evans Notch (Rt 113) has no services along the way.
  • Errol has limited food options — treat it as a snack stop and save the real meal for Berlin.
  • ~140–150 miles — a shorter day, but pad extra time here: mountain roads run slower than flat backroads.
  • Book: Colonial Motel, North Conway ↗ — request the 2 Queen Beds room.

Wolfeboro & Home

North Conway → Wolfeboro, NH → 12 Lincoln St, Waltham

Why this way

  • Route south through the Lakes Region to Wolfeboro is entirely fresh road — different corridor from Day 1's approach through Milford, so nothing gets repeated across the whole weekend.
  • Shortest, lowest-pressure day — built-in buffer ahead of the 8–9pm return deadline.

Stops along the way

  • North Conwaygas before departure
  • Wolfeboro, NHlunch
  • Rochester / Epping, NHtop off
  • 12 Lincoln St, Walthamarrival

Logistics to keep in mind

  • ~140 miles — with an 8–9am start you should land around 5–6:30pm, well ahead of the 8–9pm target.
  • That buffer is useful margin, not a reason to dawdle — Sunday evening traffic approaching the Boston area can eat into it.
  • Top off in Rochester or Epping so you're not hunting for gas in denser traffic closer to the city.

Packing the T-Bag

  • Bottom / center, near the seat: tools, tire kit, toiletries — the heavy stuff, kept low and centered so it doesn't affect handling.
  • Top roll: clothing — light, compressible, fine to sit higher.
  • Outer pocket: rain gear, snacks, charging cable — anything you want at a gas stop without digging through the whole bag.
