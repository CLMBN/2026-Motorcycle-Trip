A three-day, non-highway loop built around a strict 2-hour-leg cadence — 3 riding legs a day (breakfast → lunch → dinner), each capped around 55–60 miles at realistic twisty-backroad pace (~28–30 mph average, not car-routing speed). Covers the Kancamagus, Hurricane Mountain Road, and the Maine shore road through Yarmouth and Freeport — with no road repeated across the three days.

Packing the T-Bag

  • Bottom / center, near the seat: tools, tire kit, toiletries — the heavy stuff, kept low and centered so it doesn't affect handling.
  • Top roll: clothing — light, compressible, fine to sit higher.
  • Outer pocket: rain gear, snacks, charging cable — anything you want at a gas stop without digging through the whole bag.
